A Google Maps data extraction Actor that automates Google Maps searches and scrolling to produce structured place records and enriched business intelligence. It scrapes business metadata (names, categories, addresses, geocoordinates), contact details (phone, website, company-derived emails and social links), operational data (opening hours, popular times histograms, price bracket, menus, reservation/booking links), ratings and review content with review metadata and reviewer info, images and image counts, location geometry (points, polygons, multipolygons, radius-based areas), and venue-type specifics for hotels and restaurants. The Actor supports flexible search inputs (search queries, categories, coordinates, direct place URLs/IDs), custom geographic areas defined via GeoJSON, site-level company contact enrichment, person-level leads enrichment (work names, titles, profiles, emails), social media profile enrichment (platform profile details and metrics), email verification, and an AI-powered competitor analysis add-on that generates per-place analysis, comparative ranking, aspect-by-aspect comparisons, sentiment-backed strengths/weaknesses, and an interactive map visualization. Outputs are organized as structured place records with arrays for reviews, images, social profiles and enrichment data suitable for lead generation, competitive research, and market analysis.

## Use cases

- Generate qualified business leads by extracting business metadata, company contact details, and enriched person-level leads.  
- Track competitors across regions with ratings, review counts, review-backed insights, and comparative analysis.  
- Perform market analysis and benchmarking by aggregating business density, ratings distribution, price brackets, and popular times.  
- Discover high-value locations and partnership opportunities by filtering top-rated or high-volume venues.  
- Automate repetitive local research workflows to keep business datasets current and consistent.
